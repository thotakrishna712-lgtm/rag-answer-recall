# Wire Transfer Operations Standard

## 1. Scope
This standard applies to all domestic and international wire transfers initiated through retail branches and digital channels.

## 2. Dual Control Approval
Any wire transfer above $10,000 requires dual control approval by two authorised staff members. The second approver must not be the initiator of the transfer. Approvals are recorded in the payments platform with both user identifiers.

## 3. Cut-off Times
Domestic wires received before 4:00 PM Eastern are processed the same business day. International wires must be submitted before 2:00 PM Eastern for same-day release.

## 4.2 Audit Log Retention
Wire transfer audit logs must be retained for 7 years in immutable storage. Logs are encrypted with customer-managed keys and access is reviewed quarterly.
