# AML Transaction Monitoring Procedure

## 1. Alert Triage
Level 1 analysts must disposition every transaction monitoring alert within 10 business days of generation. Alerts that cannot be closed are escalated to a Level 2 investigator.

## 2. Suspicious Activity Report Filing
A suspicious activity report must be filed within 30 calendar days of the initial detection of suspicious activity. The filing deadline may be extended once by 30 days when no suspect is identified.

## 3. Tipping Off
Staff must never disclose to a customer that a suspicious activity report has been filed or is under consideration.
